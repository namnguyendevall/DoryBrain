# H002: Threshold vs Uniform Random under Scarcity

**Hypothesis:**
A threshold-based decision policy produces fewer invalid actions (Constraint Violations) and yields a higher survival rate than a uniformly random policy under deterministic scarcity constraints.

**Independent Variable:** 
Decision Policy (Threshold vs. Uniform Random)

**Dependent Variables:**
1. Survival Ticks
2. Constraint Violations

**Falsification Criteria:**
Reject H002 if:
1. The mean survival difference ≤ ε (where ε = 1.0 ticks).
2. The 95% Confidence Intervals of the Survival Ticks overlap.
3. The Uniform Random policy produces a higher mean survival or a lower mean Constraint Violation count under scarcity conditions.
