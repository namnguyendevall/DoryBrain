# Finding 009: Continuous Latent State Estimators Do Not Break Policy Equivalence

## Overview
Batch 0006 evaluated E007, an actor equipped with a **Continuous Latent State Estimator**. Unlike previous actors that relied on finite history or reactive fixed levels, E007 continuously updated a *belief* about the environmental dynamics trend ($\Delta$resource) using an Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (EWMA, $\alpha=0.2$). E007 used this belief to modulate its preference for action (e.g., buffering its threshold when it believed the environment was depleting). 

## 4-Tier Evaluation

### Tier 1 — Implementation
- **Question**: Does the actor maintain a continuous latent state?
- **Metric**: `ewma_delta` tracking in internal state.
- **Expected**: YES. E007 successfully implemented the EWMA state variable.

### Tier 2 — Engagement
- **Question**: Does the estimator respond to environmental changes?
- **Metrics**: 
  - `trend_correlation` (Pearson correlation between estimated trend and true resource delta)
  - `delta_MAE` (Mean Absolute Estimation Error)
- **Result**: **YES**. At the critical boundary (`gain_01`), E007's estimator strongly correlated with the true environment (`Corr = 0.60`, `MAE = 0.08`), whereas the isomorphic control (E007_Control) had no meaningful correlation (`Corr = -0.06`, `MAE = 1.00`). The actor successfully formed a grounded belief about the environment.

### Tier 3 — Behavioral Change
- **Question**: Does the estimated trend alter the trajectory?
- **Metrics**: Transient work rate and survival in extreme conditions (`gain_00`).
- **Result**: **YES**. In highly depleting conditions (`gain_00`), E007's belief that the environment was deteriorating led it to successfully suppress its work rate (from E000 baseline $0.0952 \to 0.0400$). This conservation behavior marginally extended its average survival from 21 ticks (baseline/control) to 25 ticks.

### Tier 4 — Boundary Shift
- **Question**: Does the Critical Recovery Threshold (CRT) move?
- **Metric**: CRT across constraint ladders.
- **Result**: **NO**. Despite accurately recognizing the deteriorating trend and altering its transient behavior to conserve energy, E007 still collapsed into the exact same Comatose State at the critical boundary (`gain_01`). It survived for 100 ticks but yielded a $0.00$ steady-state work rate. The CRT remained entirely invariant.

## Conclusion & Implications
We reject the alternative hypothesis and uphold $H_0$: **Within the evaluated environment class and estimator configuration, continuous latent state estimation does not measurably shift the CRT.**

This finding reveals a profound insight: **Information about the environment is not equivalent to control authority over the environment.** Even when the actor explicitly *knows* (via grounded belief) that the environment is starving it, it lacks the expressive capacity to sequence actions that break the energetic deadlock. The boundary remains dictated by physics, not by the actor's epistemological state.
