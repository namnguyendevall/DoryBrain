# Finding 011: Multi-step Planning Does Not Shift the Critical Recovery Threshold (CRT)

## Observation
Batch 0008 evaluated a depth-3 planner (E009) against the baseline (E000) and an isomorphic control (E009_Control). The planner utilizes an internal predictive model (learned strictly from real experience) to evaluate the utility of 3-step action sequences, preferring sequences that maximize `resource + 10.0 * num_WORK` while surviving, and strictly tie-breaking in favor of `REST`.

Despite possessing the capability to evaluate future consequences and actively plan a sequence of actions, E009 **failed to shift the Critical Recovery Threshold (CRT)** on all three constraint ladders (`ladder_work`, `ladder_gain`, `ladder_decay`). 

In fact, at the boundary of `ladder_work` (`cost_09`, where work cost = 6.0 and rest gain = 4.0), E009 actively refused to work. A sequence of three rests yields a net resource change of +9.0, while a sequence of one work and two rests yields a net resource change of -1.0, plus a work bonus of 10.0 (total utility 9.0). Due to the pre-registered tie-breaking rule (`Prefer REST`), the planner correctly deduced that working offered no temporal advantage within its horizon and chose to enter the Comatose state permanently (SSWorkR = 0.00), whereas the reactive baseline (E000) managed to squeeze out marginal work (SSWorkR = 0.08).

## Capability Framework Assessment

1. **Was the capability implemented?** Yes. E009 executes a full binary tree search (14 transitions evaluated per step).
2. **Was the capability learned?** Yes. Parameter error is extremely low (`ParamErr ~1.0`), showing accurate environmental model acquisition.
3. **Was the capability engaged?** Yes. Diagnostics show `search_nodes = 13.7` (near the maximum 14, slightly pruned by death states).
4. **Did the behavior change?** Yes. E009 alters its local trajectories and actively chooses not to work at `cost_09` due to tie-breaking, proving the planner is driving decisions.
5. **Did the CRT shift?** **NO.** The phase boundary remained fundamentally invariant.

## Interpretation
The inability of E009 to push past the CRT demonstrates that the Comatose phase boundary is an **asymptotic constraint of the environment's physics**, not merely a limitation of the agent's foresight or temporal credit assignment. The agent possesses full knowledge of the local physics (Prediction) and the ability to search for optimal future control sequences (Planning), yet it remains bounded by the fundamental thermodynamic limits of the environment (`rest_gain <= passive_decay`).

As conceptualized in F009, this confirms that:
`Future consequence evaluation (Planning) does not equate to leverage over the asymptotic phase boundary.`

The constraint is absolute.
