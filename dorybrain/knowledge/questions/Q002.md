# Q002: Decision Policies under Scarcity

**Question:** 
Does deterministic decision-making consistently outperform uniform random decision-making across multiple seeds under a constrained scarcity environment?

**Background:**
In Q001/E000, both a threshold-based deterministic policy and a uniform random policy survived 100 ticks without generating any constraint violations. This indicates that the default system constraints were too relaxed to differentiate the adaptive value of the policies. 
To isolate the value of the decision policy, we must increase the systemic pressure (e.g., lower resource recovery or higher resource drain).
