# Finding F012: Hidden Resource Storage (Action Expansion) Does Not Shift the Phase Boundary

## 1. Context
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Experiment**: Batch 0009 (E010 vs E000 vs E010_Control)
**Hypothesis**: Giving the agent a larger action space (`STORE` and `RETRIEVE`) to manage hidden reserves (`bank`) will allow it to weather starvation periods and functionally shift the Critical Recovery Threshold (CRT).

## 2. Experimental Setup
* **Action Space**: `['rest', 'work', 'store', 'retrieve']`.
* **Bank Mechanic**: Non-decaying hidden resource buffer.
* **Physics Modification**: None (Energy conservation strictly verified, mean error = 0.0). `STORE` costs 2.0 resource to add 2.0 to the bank. `RETRIEVE` moves up to 5.0 from the bank back to resource.
* **Policy (E010)**: Reactive. Store when `resource > 50`, retrieve when `resource < 15`, otherwise fallback to `E000` (threshold = 20).
* **Control (E010_Control)**: Action taken based strictly on clock cycle, ignoring resource state.

## 3. Results

### Energy Conservation Check
* Verified across all scenarios. Mean conservation error = **0.0**. The action space expansion introduced no "free energy".

### Boundary Shift Evaluation
Across all 3 constraint ladders (`ladder_work`, `ladder_gain`, `ladder_decay`), the Steady-State Work Rate (SSWorkR) of E010 mirrored E000 exactly, collapsing to near 0 at the exact same constraint points.

**Example: `ladder_work`**
* `cost_05`: E000 = 0.14 | E010 = 0.14
* `cost_09`: E000 = 0.08 | E010 = 0.08

**Example: `ladder_gain`**
* `gain_04`: E000 = 0.38 | E010 = 0.38
* `gain_05`: E000 = 0.46 | E010 = 0.44

## 4. Conclusion
**Case A Confirmed**: Resource buffering increases temporal robustness (by shifting resources away from passive decay during abundant periods) but **does not alter the environmental sustainability boundary**. 

Better energy management and action expansion without causal intervention is insufficient to overcome the thermodynamic reality of `rest_gain <= passive_decay`. The boundary remains invariant to the agent's action space expressivity when that expressivity does not alter the underlying physics.
